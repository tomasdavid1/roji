## So why aren't people converting?

Here's my honest read after looking at the data and the data alone:

### The actual bottleneck is `/cart/` → `/checkout/`

Out of the 6 confirmed real days post-fix:
- 71 real users walked into the store
- ~10 viewed a PDP
- 2 added to cart
- 2 viewed the cart
- **0 clicked "Proceed to checkout"**

We landed cart fixes (visual layout, post-ATC banner, button hierarchy)
and the checkout reassurance card on May 10. They're live. Yet none
of the 4 cart sessions we got over the next 5 days clicked through.

### Three plausible explanations, ordered by my confidence

**1. Sample size: 4 cart sessions is too small to conclude anything.**
We need ~20-30 cart sessions before "0% conversion" means anything
real. At our current rate of 0-1 cart sessions per day we'd need
20-30 more days to hit that.

**2. The "Reserve Order" gateway is what's killing the click.**
We added the reassurance card explaining "nothing is charged today,
you'll get a payment link by email" — but a user who's never seen
this pattern from any other store might still feel like "this is
weird, they don't process payments?" and bail. The Welland CA user
who ATC'd on May 10 is the most interesting data point: they got
to ATC, viewed cart, and then left. Did the reassurance card fail
to convince them, or was the reservation gateway just genuinely too
strange? We can't know without either (a) talking to them, or (b)
A/B testing a normal-payment alternative.

**3. The calculator-intent traffic genuinely won't buy from us yet.**
These are mostly researchers / biohackers who came to do dose math.
They might be future buyers, but for now they're just using the
free tool. The cart is showing them prices, then they think "yeah
but I'd buy from my usual vendor" and leave.

### The instrumentation gap that's holding us back

We have a real visibility hole: **GA4 isn't firing `view_item` on
PDPs.** That means:
- We can't compute "PDP view → ATC" rate per product.
- We can't see how long users spend on PDPs.
- We can't tell if a specific product has unusually-bad PDP UX.

Wiring this in is a small instrumentation task. Worth doing.

### Also: there might be a `begin_checkout` tracking bug

We renamed `checkout_view` → `begin_checkout` on May 7 (Fix #4).
The script counts both event names. **Both are zero across 6 days.**
Either nobody clicked through to /checkout/ (consistent with /cart/
being the wall), OR the event isn't firing properly. To distinguish:
on a fresh device + Chrome DevTools, click "Proceed to Checkout" on
/cart/ and watch the network tab for a `gtag` call with
`event: begin_checkout`. If that fires → confirmed bottleneck is
/cart/. If it doesn't → instrumentation bug.

   Brazilian / Dominican / Suriname clicks. Should save ~5-10% of

   yourself on a non-Rio device with Chrome DevTools open. If

   users is real and we need to investigate the cart→checkout
   experience deeper.

4. **Investigate the 6 chatgpt.com referrals.** Free traffic from a
   high-credibility referrer. Where do they land, what's their
   bounce rate, can we optimize whatever they're seeing?

5. **Don't change bid strategy yet.** Maximize Conversions still
   wants 30+ conversions. We're at 0 real (or 2 false) for the week.
   Stay on Maximize Clicks. You said "Wednesday night" for the next
   strategic checkpoint — that gives us 5 more days of cleaner data.

6. **Consider lowering the budget further** to $20-25/day. We're
   spending $30/day on traffic that converts at <1%. If we still
   spend $30/day with 0 conversions for another 5 days that's $150
   more wasted. Going to $20 only loses ~30% of impression volume
