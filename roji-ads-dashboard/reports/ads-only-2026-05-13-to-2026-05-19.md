## What the numbers say about the May 15 fixes

**Pre-fix (5/13–5/14, $30/day budget, leaky geo):** avg 3.96% CTR,
$0.92 CPC.

**Post-fix (5/15–5/18, $20/day budget, presence-only geo):** avg
6.44% CTR, $0.83 CPC.

That's a **+63% CTR uplift and -10% CPC** — exactly what we'd expect
when removing presence-or-interest impressions in LatAm/EU that
padded the impression count without producing clicks. Fewer wasted
impressions, more focused auctions, lower CPC.

**Click volume per dollar** went from 1.08 clicks/$ → 1.32 clicks/$
(+22%). Same money, more engaged users.

**Conversions = 0** in the Ads platform for the post-fix window.
Without GA4 we can't see if there were ATCs that just haven't
attribution-propagated yet (24-48h lag), but the Ads-side conversion
counter has been at 0 for 4 days. We'll know once GA4 token is
refreshed.
